Your writing does not need an audience. A thought from today, a view worth remembering, a feeling you have not quite sorted out. Miroven is making a place for those records.

## A journal that starts on your device

Miroven works locally first. You can write and search entries on your current device without signing in. Records and photos are encrypted on your device first.

If you want to continue on another device, connect an account to sync encrypted records. Sync is optional.

## A way to keep your records

As your journal grows, keeping it safe matters too. Store file backups and your recovery code separately to prepare for cleared browser storage or a lost device.

We also explain the scope and limits of encryption openly. See our [Security guide](/en/security/) for details.

## Start in the official app

This official website provides product information and guides. Start your own journal in the [official Miroven app](https://miroven-app.mksdr.bid). If you are new, visit [Help](/en/help/) to get started.
